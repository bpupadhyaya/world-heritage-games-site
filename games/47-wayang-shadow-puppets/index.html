Wayang Shadow Puppets

Also known as Wayang kulit

Wayang kulit is the shadow theatre of Java and Bali. A single puppeteer, the dalang, moves cut and painted buffalo-hide figures behind a lit cloth and speaks every voice, while a gamelan orchestra plays beside the cloth.

How to play

  1. Watch each scene as the shadows play it on the lit screen.
  2. Tap a choice, or press its number, to decide what happens next.
  3. When the old farmer asks a riddle, pick the answer you think is right.
  4. Wit, goodwill and water build as you choose; your ending gives one to three stars.
Rules at a glance
  • The tale branches several ways and has three possible endings.
  • Patient, kind and clever choices earn wisdom points; shouting and shortcuts earn none.
  • Some choices make you late or unkind, and those cannot be undone.
  • The riddle is answered once; a wrong answer is not fatal but costs wit.
  • Three stars need wit, goodwill and no delays.

About the tradition

Wayang kulit has been performed on Java and Bali for many centuries and is recognised internationally as a masterpiece of living heritage. Puppeteers, musicians and puppet-makers keep it alive, often within families. This tale is an original village story in the style of its clown-servant comedies.

Origins

Usually placed in the era of Classical Worlds (about 1000 BCE to 500 CE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Southeast Asia and the Islands.
