Pallanguzhi

Also known as Pallankuzhi · Vamana Guntalu · Kuzhipara

Mancala sowing game on a 14-pit board with shells or seeds, played at home in the south.

Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of India.

Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of South Asia.
