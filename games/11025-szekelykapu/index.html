Székelykapu

Also known as Szekely gate

Carve a village gate: a gabled roof with a sun disc, three posts with ball caps and a slatted leaf.

How to play

  1. The lit piece in its frame shows what to make.
  2. Drag across the block to take wood away; two chisels, fine and broad. Zoom in to work close and use the Hand to move the view.
  3. Rub the Wax cloth over the finished piece, then press Check.
Rules at a glance
  • Removing a cell inside the shape costs as much as leaving one outside it.
  • Undo takes back one stroke. Waxing is for the finish and does not change the score.
  • Three stars for 88 or more, two stars for 65 and one star for 40.
  • Watch and learn carves and waxes a whole round for you.

About the tradition

In the Szekler Land of Transylvania, gates of oak combine a large gate for carts and a small gate for people under one roof. Posts end in carved balls, and tulips, rosettes and suns are cut into the beams.

Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Northern and Eastern Europe.
