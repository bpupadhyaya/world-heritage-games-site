Korfball

Also known as Korfbal

Two baskets stand on tall posts with no backboard behind them, so every shot has to arrive from the front and drop cleanly through. A mixed team of women and men plays, and the shot from distance is the pride of the sport.

How to play

  1. Press and hold to wind up: the ring shows where the ball will come down, and it drifts a little with your hand.
  2. Release when the ring sits over the basket.
  3. Eight shots make a round and there are two rounds.
Rules at a glance
  • Straight through the middle of the basket scores 3; in off the rim scores 2.
  • A ball that hits the rim and bounces away scores nothing.
  • There is no backboard, so a shot must be thrown on a true line.

About the tradition

Korfball was designed in the Netherlands at the start of the twentieth century as a team game for women and men together, with baskets on tall posts and no backboard.

Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Celtic and Atlantic Europe.
