Gankogui

Also known as Gangokui

The gankogui is a double bell of forged iron: a large and a small cone joined by a curved handle. Struck with a stick, it gives a high and a low note and carries the steady timeline of Ewe drum music.

How to play

  1. Four places are lit: the Low bell and the High bell, and the edge of each mouth.
  2. Strike the bell a closing ring marks. The edge gives a short, hard tick.
  3. Keep a steady pattern: the bell is the clock that every other player follows.
Rules at a glance
  • A stroke on the beat is perfect, a near one is good, and extra strokes cost points.
  • Each pattern repeats twice.
  • The round ends when the last stroke has been judged; the score is the share of the best possible.

About the tradition

A bell pattern repeats unchanged through a whole piece, and the drummers set their rhythms against it. The two cones give the pattern its high and low voices.

Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Africa: Sahel, West and Central.
