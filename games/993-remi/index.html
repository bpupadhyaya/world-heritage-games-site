Remi

Also known as Реми

Rummy in two forms: the open version lets you meld on the table, the closed version only once the hand can finish.

Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of North Macedonia, Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina.

Origins

Usually placed in the era of Age of Contact and Craft (about 1500 to 1800), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Mediterranean.
