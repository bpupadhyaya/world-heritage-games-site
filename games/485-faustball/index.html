Faustball

Also known as fist ball

Two teams of five strike a ball with a closed fist or forearm across a line stretched between posts, on a grass field. Here you serve from the back line into the far half, reading the breeze and choosing the zone to land in.

How to play

  1. Watch the wind flag and the dotted flight path.
  2. Stop the first sweep to set the direction of your fist serve.
  3. Stop the second sweep to set the strength, and the ball sails over the line.
  4. Land it in the middle of the far half for the most points. Two rounds of five serves.
Rules at a glance
  • The ball is struck only with a closed fist or the forearm.
  • The serve must clear the line between the posts and land inside the far half.
  • Landing in the middle zone scores 3, the next zone 2, the near and back zones 1.
  • A ball landing outside the side lines or beyond the back line scores nothing.
  • The wind changes at the start of each round and bends the flight.
  • A round is five serves; the score is the total over two rounds.

About the tradition

Fistball is played mostly in Germany, Austria and Switzerland and in Brazil, with roots that reach back to ball games of Southern Europe. German gymnastics clubs drafted its first rules in the 1890s, and it is still played on village and club lawns.

Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Northern and Eastern Europe.
