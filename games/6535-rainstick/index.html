Rainstick

A rainstick is a long dried cactus tube filled with pebbles or seeds and lined inside with spines. When it is tilted, the seeds trickle down past the spines and the tube sounds like falling rain.

How to play

  1. Four places are lit: the Tap on the end cap, Soft rain, Heavy rain and Last drops.
  2. Tilt the tube at the place a closing ring marks. The seeds run down inside and the sound pours out.
  3. Each rain has its own length, so wait for it to finish before the next one starts.
Rules at a glance
  • A stroke on the beat is perfect, a near one is good, and extra strokes cost points.
  • Each pattern repeats twice.
  • The round ends when the last stroke has been judged; the score is the share of the best possible.

About the tradition

Rainsticks are made from cactus that has dried out; the spines are pressed into the wall of the tube in a spiral. The tube is decorated with painted bands.

Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin.
