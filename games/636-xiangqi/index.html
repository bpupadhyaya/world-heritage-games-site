Xiangqi

象棋 Xiangqi · Elephant chess · Chinese chess

Two armies face off across a river on a 9x10 grid; each side has a general confined to a palace and the winner traps the enemy general.

Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of China, Taiwan.

Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of China.
