Checkers

Also known as Draughts · pool checkers

Common bar and park game in the Caribbean; pool-checkers (Wikipedia) is regionally associated.

Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Bahamas, United States.

Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of The Americas: Caribbean.
