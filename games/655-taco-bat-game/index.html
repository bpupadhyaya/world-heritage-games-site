Encyclopedia ID 655

Taco bat game

Also known as Juego del Taco

Minimal-equipment bat-and-ball street game that resembles street cricket and baseball.

Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Cuba.

Origins

Usually placed in the era of Age of Contact and Craft (about 1500 to 1800), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of The Americas: Caribbean.
