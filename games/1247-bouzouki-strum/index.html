Bouzouki Strum

Also known as Μπουζούκι

Pluck paired steel strings in a quick tremolo as notes fall, shifting between rebetiko scale modes.

Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Greece.

Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Mediterranean.
