Bohemian glass making

Blow a heavy-footed vase of clear crystal, flash its lip with ruby glass and see the flutes and diamond cuts and a gold rim catch the light.

How to play

  1. Match the shape in the frame.
  2. Drag across each row to set its width.
  3. Tap the bands to dip them in ruby glass.
  4. Press Check.
Rules at a glance
  • The vase has a heavy foot, a straight body and a flared mouth.
  • Shape counts for 70 of the 100 points and the colour of the bands for 30.
  • Three stars for 88 or more, two stars for 65 and one star for 40. Watch and learn blows a whole vase for you.

About the tradition

Bohemian glassmakers are known for clear crystal that is cut with stone wheels into flutes and diamonds and engraved, and for thin flashes of ruby glass over the clear.

Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Northern and Eastern Europe.
