Malarstwo Zalipia

Also known as Tradycyjne malarstwo Zalipia · Zalipie painted cottages

Paint a garland of flowers on a white wall: stems and leaves, a red poppy, blue daisies and bells, a dotted frame.

How to play

  1. The lit panel shows the finished painting.
  2. Tap a colour to dip the brush; drag along the guide for a line, or tap a shape to fill it.
  3. Each step has its own lit strokes and shapes; Undo takes back the last one.
Rules at a glance
  • Steps run in order; each must be finished before the next.
  • Score: strokes on the guide in the right colour and shapes filled in the right colour.
  • Three stars for 88 or more, two stars for 65 and one star for 40.
  • Watch and learn carves and waxes a whole round for you.

About the tradition

In the village of Zalipie in southern Poland, women paint flowers on house walls, wells and kennels. Bold poppies, daisies and bells are laid on white lime and often outlined in black.

Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Northern and Eastern Europe.
