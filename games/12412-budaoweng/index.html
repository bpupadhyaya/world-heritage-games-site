Budaoweng

Also known as 不倒翁 · Bu dao weng · Tumbler · tumbler doll

Mould a pear-shaped tumbler, paint its gold robe with a red roundel and its black cap, then draw the smiling face.

How to play

  1. Match the doll in the frame.
  2. Drag across each row to set its width.
  3. Tap the bands to paint the robe and cap.
  4. Press Paint face and tap each mark.
  5. Press Check.
Rules at a glance
  • The doll is a pear: a heavy round base, a narrow chest, a small head and a flat cap.
  • Its weight is low, so it rights itself every time it is tipped.
  • Shape counts for 55 of the 100 points: how closely each row of the doll matches the lit outline.
  • The bands count for 25 points: each band must be the right colour.
  • The finishing touches count for 20 points: tap every glowing mark to paint the face.
  • Three stars for 88 or more, two stars for 65 and one star for 40. Watch and learn paints a whole doll for you.

About the tradition

Budaoweng means 'old man who does not fall'. These tumblers have been made in clay and papier-mache for centuries, usually as a gentle figure in an official's cap and robe.

Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of China.
