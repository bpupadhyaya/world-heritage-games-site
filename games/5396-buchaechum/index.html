Buchaechum

Also known as 부채춤 · fan dance

A Korean dance in which dancers open, sweep and close large painted folding fans, so that the fans themselves turn into flowers, waves and wings.

How to play

  1. Four places are lit beside the dancer: Open fans, Sweep, Bloom and Close.
  2. A ring closes on a place when that movement is due. Tap the place as the ring closes and the fans answer.
  3. Start with the open and close, then the waves, then the flower round.
Rules at a glance
  • A tap right on the beat is perfect, a near one is good, and taps with no ring open cost points.
  • Each pattern is played twice through; every pattern is counted in fours.
  • Patterns are simplified teaching figures in the style of the dance, not choreography to copy.

About the tradition

The fan dance grew from court and folk dances and became one of the best-known stage dances of Korea. Dancers in long-sleeved jackets and full skirts hold a fan in each hand and move so that the painted fans, opened and closed together, make shapes of peonies, waves and birds.

Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Japan and Korea.
