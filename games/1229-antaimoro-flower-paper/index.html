Antaimoro flower paper

Spread soft bark pulp on a mould, press fresh flowers and leaves into it, and brush the dried sheet flat.

How to play

  1. Press Pour to put the pulp on the mould.
  2. Rub the heap out until it lies evenly.
  3. Press Lift.
  4. Brush the wrinkles flat.
  5. Press Check.
Rules at a glance
  • The pulp is poured in a heap; rubbing spreads it to every corner.
  • Evenness is the score; the flowers are pressed in afterwards.
  • Fibre that is moved stays where the hand leaves it; the heap never gets lighter, so every stroke spreads it further.
  • The form of the sheet counts for 60 points and the finish for 40.
  • Three stars for 88 or more, two stars for 65 and one star for 40. Watch and learn makes a whole sheet for you.

About the tradition

Antaimoro paper is made in south-east Madagascar from the bark of the avoha shrub. Fresh flowers and leaves are pressed into the wet pulp and dry into the sheet.

Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Africa: Great Lakes and Indian Ocean.
