Tarakasi silver filigree

Also known as ତାରକସି

Twist thin silver wire into curls and fix them into a frame to complete flower and leaf designs.

Heritage note

A tradition shared by communities in many places.

Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin.
