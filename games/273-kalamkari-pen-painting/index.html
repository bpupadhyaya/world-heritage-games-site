Encyclopedia ID 273

Kalamkari pen painting

Also known as కలంకారి

Draw natural motifs with a bamboo kalam on cloth, then fill in them using the mordant-and-dye steps in the right order.

Heritage note

A tradition shared by communities in many places.

Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin.
