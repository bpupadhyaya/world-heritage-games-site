Lokta paper-making

Also known as लोक्ता कागज

Pour boiled and beaten lokta pulp on a mould floating in a tank, spread it with your hand until it lies evenly, then pound the dry sheet smooth with a mallet.

How to play

  1. Press Pour, then spread the pulp across the mould with your hand.
  2. Press Lift when the fibre lies evenly.
  3. Pound each zone of the sheet as it lights.
  4. Press Check.
Rules at a glance
  • Push the pulp from the thick heap to the bare edges.
  • Fibre that is moved stays where the hand leaves it; the heap never gets lighter, so every stroke spreads it further.
  • The form of the sheet counts for 60 points and the finish for 40.
  • Three stars for 88 or more, two stars for 65 and one star for 40. Watch and learn makes a whole sheet for you.

About the tradition

Lokta is a strong paper made in the Himalayan foothills of Nepal from the inner bark of a shrub. The pulp is spread by hand on a mould floating in a tank and the sheets are dried in the sun.

Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of South Asia.
