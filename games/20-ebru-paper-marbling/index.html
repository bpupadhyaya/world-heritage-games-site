Ebru paper marbling

Also known as Ebru (Turkish) · Abru

Float drops of paint on a tray of thickened water, draw a needle through them to pull flowers, and lay a sheet of paper on top to take the pattern.

How to play

  1. The pattern in the frame shows what to make.
  2. Tap the tray to drop the colour the ring shows.
  3. Draw the comb or the needle along the lit lines.
  4. Press Lay paper to print.
Rules at a glance
  • Each new drop pushes the earlier drops outward, so the order matters.
  • Three stars for 88 or more, two stars for 65 and one star for 40.
  • Doing nothing scores nothing. Watch and learn plays a whole round for you.

About the tradition

Ebru is the Turkish art of marbling paper. Colours are floated on a tray of thickened water, drawn into flowers and waves with a needle or a comb, and printed by laying a sheet of paper on top.

Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Mesopotamia, Levant and Arabia.
