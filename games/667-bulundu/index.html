Bulundu

Also known as Luba ball game

Ball game of the Luba, listed in Wikipedia's African traditional games list.

Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of DR Congo.

Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Sahel, West and Central.
