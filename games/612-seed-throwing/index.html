Seed throwing

Also known as Bos Angkunh

Players throw a seed at target seeds on the ground; longer versions add further stages of skill.

Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Cambodia.

Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Southeast Asia and the Islands.
