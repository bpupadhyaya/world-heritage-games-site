Ice Canoe Race

Also known as Course en canot a glace

A canoe with steel runners on a river of broken ice: crews paddle open water and push across the floes, and the strokes decide who gets through.

How to play

  1. Watch the ring close on the pad and strike the moment it meets the pad.
  2. Alternate sides with every beat to keep the canoe moving through the open water.
  3. Floes slow everyone down: hold your rhythm on the rough patches and keep going.
Rules at a glance
  • A stroke on the beat gives a full push; off-beat strokes spend strength and give almost nothing.
  • Rough water shrinks the timing window.
  • Stars: first with a steady rhythm for three; first alone for two; on the podium for one.

About the tradition

Teams race winter canoes over the icy St Lawrence, paddling the channels and dragging the hull across floes. The canoe wears runners so that it can slide as well as float.

Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of The Americas: North America.
