Tuk Band

Also known as Barbados

Play the bass drum, snare ('kettle'), triangle and tin flute in a marching ensemble, layering parts to strike the steady tuk beat.

Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Barbados.

Origins

Usually placed in the era of Classical Worlds (about 1000 BCE to 500 CE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of The Americas: Caribbean.
