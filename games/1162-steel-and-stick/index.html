Steel-and-stick

Also known as Çelik çomak · tipcat

Street game where a short stick is struck with a longer stick and distance is scored.

Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Turkey.

Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Mesopotamia, Levant and Arabia.
