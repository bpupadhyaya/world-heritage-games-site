Edo kimekomi doll

Also known as 江戸木目込人形 · Edo kimekomi ningyo

Carve a doll from a paulownia core, tuck patterned cloth into its grooves, wrap the gold sash and paint the face.

How to play

  1. Match the doll in the frame.
  2. Drag across each row to set its width.
  3. Tap the bands to paint the cloth.
  4. Press Paint face and tap each mark.
  5. Press Check.
Rules at a glance
  • The body is a wide hem, a narrow sash, sloping shoulders and a round head.
  • Cloth is pressed into carved grooves; the dark lines between the panels are the grooves.
  • Shape counts for 55 of the 100 points: how closely each row of the doll matches the lit outline.
  • The bands count for 25 points: each band must be the right colour.
  • The finishing touches count for 20 points: tap every glowing mark to paint the face.
  • Three stars for 88 or more, two stars for 65 and one star for 40. Watch and learn paints a whole doll for you.

About the tradition

Kimekomi means 'tucked in': a carved wooden or paulownia-paste body is cut with fine grooves and scraps of silk are pressed into them with a thin blade, with no stitching.

Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Japan and Korea.
