Adji-boto

Adji-boto · Saramaccan mancala

Mancala game of the Saramaccan Maroons; played for ten days after a death and boards are made only by widows.

Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Suriname.

Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of The Americas: Caribbean.
