Word Game

Also known as Word Game Deluxe

Players study a set of words, then are tested on them in short timed rounds that return to the words they missed.

Heritage note

An Arcforge original, created for the World Heritage Games collection rather than drawn from one tradition.

Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today).
