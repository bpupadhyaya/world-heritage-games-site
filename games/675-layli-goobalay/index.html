Encyclopedia ID 675

Layli Goobalay

Also known as Closing the holes

Somali mancala dug in the ground, historically with dried camel dung as pieces.

Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Djibouti, Somalia.

Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Nile and the Horn.
