Maamoul Cookie Moulding

Also known as ma'amoul · معمول

Maamoul Cookie Moulding is a crafts and making tradition from Lebanon, Syria.

Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Lebanon, Syria, Jordan.

Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Mesopotamia, Levant and Arabia.
