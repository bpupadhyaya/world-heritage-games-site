Table football

Also known as Futbolín · Foosball

A tabletop version of football with rods of figures that players spin and slide to strike a small ball.

How it is played

Players turn rods holding small figures to hit the ball past the opposing figures into the goal.

Heritage note

A tradition shared by communities in many places.

Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain.
