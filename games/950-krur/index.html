Krur

Also known as Karur

Small two-row mancala from the Hassaniya Sahara with relay sowing.

Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Morocco.

Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Mediterranean.
