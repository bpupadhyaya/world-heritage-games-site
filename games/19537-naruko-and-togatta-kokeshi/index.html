Naruko and Togatta kokeshi

Also known as 鳴子こけし・遠刈田こけし · Naruko Kokeshi · Togatta Kokeshi

Turn a wooden doll with a round head and a straight body, then paint its rings, its hair and a chrysanthemum on the chest.

How to play

  1. Match the doll in the frame.
  2. Drag across each row of wood to set its width.
  3. Tap the bands to paint the rings.
  4. Press Finish, then tap each glowing mark to paint the face.
  5. Press Check.
Rules at a glance
  • Rows are narrow at the neck, full at the head and plain along the body.
  • Shape counts for 55 of the 100 points: how closely each row of the doll matches the lit outline.
  • The bands count for 25 points: each band must be the right colour.
  • The finishing touches count for 20 points: tap every glowing mark to paint it.
  • Three stars for 88 or more, two stars for 65 and one star for 40. Watch and learn paints a whole doll for you.

About the tradition

Kokeshi are turned wooden dolls from the hot-spring villages of the Tohoku region of Japan. Naruko dolls have a head that twists in its socket with a soft squeak, and are painted with a chrysanthemum and rings.

Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Japan and Korea.
