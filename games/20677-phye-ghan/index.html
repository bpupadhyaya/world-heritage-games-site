Phye Ghan

Also known as फ्ये गँ: · Phye Gan · Wind-activated bell

A small iron bell hangs from a bamboo beam outside a hill house. A flat wooden paddle hangs inside it, and when the wind moves the paddle, it knocks the bell and rings.

How to play

  1. Four places are lit: Lip, Waist, Shoulder and Paddle.
  2. Strike the place a closing ring marks.
  3. Let each ring fade before the next, like a breeze.
Rules at a glance
  • A stroke on the beat is perfect, a near one is good, and extra strokes cost points.
  • Each pattern repeats twice.
  • The round ends when the last stroke has been judged; the score is the share of the best possible.

About the tradition

A bell whose clapper is moved by the wind rather than by a hand rings out over the terraces (the picture shows it enlarged).

Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of South Asia.
