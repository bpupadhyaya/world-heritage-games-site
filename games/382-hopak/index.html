Hopak

Also known as Гопак · Ukrainian folk dance

Hopak is the fast, high-spirited folk dance of Ukraine, counted in two quick beats to the bar. The dancer, in wide red trousers, a sash and tall fur cap, stamps and steps, drops into a deep squat and kicks one leg out along the floor, and springs into a leap with a clap, each figure bolder than the last.

How to play

  1. Four places are lit beside the dancer: Left and Right are the stamping steps, Kick is the deep squat with one leg thrown out, and Clap is the leap with the hands meeting.
  2. A ring closes on a place when that movement is due. Tap the place as the ring closes and the dancer answers with the move.
  3. Start with the steps and stamps, add the squat kick and the clap, then try the quick round.
Rules at a glance
  • A tap right on the beat is perfect, a near one is good, and taps with no ring open cost points.
  • Each pattern is played twice through; every pattern is counted in twos.
  • Patterns are simplified teaching figures in the style of the dance, not choreography to copy.

About the tradition

Hopak grew among the Cossack communities of the Ukrainian steppe and is now danced at weddings, festivals and on stage across Ukraine. It is shown here as dance and athletic skill, never as real fighting.

Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Northern and Eastern Europe.
