Dart/spear throwing

Also known as Tiʻa

Traditional aim contests listed with footracing and spear throwing.

Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Samoa.

Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Pacific and Oceania.
