Takasaki daruma

Also known as 高崎だるま

Mould a round red papier-mache doll, paint its gold foot band and its face, then dot the eyes to finish it.

How to play

  1. Match the round doll in the frame.
  2. Drag across each row to set its width.
  3. Tap the bands to paint the foot and body.
  4. Press Dot the eyes and tap each eye.
  5. Press Check.
Rules at a glance
  • The doll is an egg: widest in the lower third and rounded at the top.
  • Shape counts for 55 of the 100 points: how closely each row of the doll matches the lit outline.
  • The bands count for 25 points: each band must be the right colour.
  • The finishing touches count for 20 points: tap every glowing mark to paint it.
  • Three stars for 88 or more, two stars for 65 and one star for 40. Watch and learn paints a whole doll for you.

About the tradition

Daruma are round, red papier-mache dolls made in Takasaki in Gunma. They are bought blank-eyed: one eye is painted in when a wish is made, and the other when it comes true.

Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Japan and Korea.
