How to play

  1. Look at the lit basket: it shows the pattern the finished basket must have.
  2. Tap a colour to lay the next stitch across the front of the ring; the gold ring marks the place, and the strip under the basket shows the row you are working on.
  3. Rows are worked from the base upward. Undo takes back the last stitch.
  4. Press Check to see your stars, or Watch and learn to see a basket woven for you.
Rules at a glance
  • Each stitch is laid in the colour you choose; rows run left to right, from the start of the work.
  • The straps, loop and flat cover are already made; you weave the splint.
  • The score counts the stitchs that match the lit target, beyond what the plain fibre colour alone would give.
  • Undo takes back the last stitch.
  • Three stars for 88 or more, two stars for 65 and one star for 40.
  • Watch and learn plays a whole round for you.

About the tradition

The pack basket is a tall basket of woven wood splint, carried on the back with leather shoulder straps and closed with a flat cover. Woodworkers, anglers and trappers of the northeastern United States packed food and gear in it. Splints are pounded from ash logs, cut to width and woven over and under.

Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of The Americas: North America.
