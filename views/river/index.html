    River of Time

    One river through six eras. Streams join it from the cradles of culture, and every boat is a game placed in its own era. This view needs JavaScript; here are the eras to browse without it.

    • Deep Pastbefore about 3000 BCE · 102 games
    • First Citiesabout 3000 to 1000 BCE · 65 games
    • Classical Worldsabout 1000 BCE to 500 CE · 102 games
    • Golden Agesabout 500 to 1500 · 125 games
    • Age of Contactabout 1500 to 1800 · 72 games
    • Modern and Living1800 to today, and traditions too continuous to date · 571 games
