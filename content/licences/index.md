# Licences and credits

## Text

All descriptions, notes and classifications are written by the World Heritage Games project and released under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). You may share and adapt them with attribution to "World Heritage Games project" and under the same licence.

## Photographs

Photographs come from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/) and keep their own licences. Every entry page shows the photographer, the licence and a link to the original file. Most are CC BY-SA or CC BY; some are in the public domain or CC0. If you reuse a photograph, follow the licence shown on its page.

## Credits

Thank you to the photographers and volunteer editors of Wikimedia Commons. Their work makes a living atlas of play possible.
