# Privacy policy for the World Heritage Games apps

Last updated: 30 October 2026.

**Short version: we collect no personal data ourselves, there are no accounts, and the apps are free to play with ads served by Google AdMob. A one-time in-app purchase removes the ads.**

## What the apps do and do not do

- The games run on your device and work offline. They need no account and no sign-in. Ads are only shown while the device is online.
- We do not collect, store on a server or sell any personal information, and we do not build a profile of you.
- The apps show advertising through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show ads and measure them, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use data from your device, such as the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions, as described in [Google's disclosures on how it uses data from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). That data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, not by us.
- Your best scores, settings (sound, text size) and whether you have removed the ads are kept only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.
- Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We receive no payment details and no information about you. The store tells the app only whether the one-time purchase that removes ads has been bought, so that Restore purchase works.
- The apps ask for no access to your contacts, photos, location or camera. Where a game offers a microphone feature it is optional, and sound is processed on the device only.

## Advertising choices

- **Consent.** In regions where the law requires it (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland) the apps show Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads load, and you can change your choice later where the app offers the privacy options.
- **iPhone and iPad.** The app asks, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, ads can still appear but will not use the advertising identifier for tracking. You can change this any time in Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.
- **Android.** You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in Settings, Google, Ads (the path varies by device).
- **Remove the ads.** One optional in-app purchase removes the ads for good. Use Restore purchase on a new device.
- **Learn more.** See [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's advertising privacy controls](https://adssettings.google.com).

## Children

The apps have no chat or social features and no links out to the web without your tap. They are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes and questions

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above. Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/bpupadhyaya/world-heritage-games-site/issues/new) or use the support page.
