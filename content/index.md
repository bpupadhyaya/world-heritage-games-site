# World Heritage Games: an atlas of play

*Every game is a window into a civilization.*

1,037 traditions from 196 countries across 19 cultural cradles and seven eras of history. Each entry has a permanent Encyclopedia ID.

## The journey through civilization

1. [Deep Past and First Games](eras/deep-past/index.md) (before about 3000 BCE): Games began with what lay to hand: knucklebones, pebbles, seeds and a patch of ground. 102 entries.
2. [River Valleys and First Cities](eras/river-valleys/index.md) (about 3000 to 1000 BCE): Games came with the first cities: boards for kings, dice for markets, and rules written down. 65 entries.
3. [Classical Worlds](eras/classical/index.md) (about 1000 BCE to 500 CE): Empires and academies turned play into sport, strategy and art. 102 entries.
4. [Crossroads and Golden Ages](eras/crossroads/index.md) (about 500 to 1500): Along the Silk Road and the sea lanes, games, instruments and crafts travelled and changed. 125 entries.
5. [Age of Contact and Craft](eras/contact/index.md) (about 1500 to 1800): Trade and empire carried pastimes across oceans while village traditions took their lasting shape. 72 entries.
6. [Modern Heritage](eras/modern/index.md) (1800 to today): Street games, national sports and revivals: heritage that people are still playing in their own lifetimes. 234 entries.
7. [Living Traditions](eras/living/index.md) (undated): Crafts, instruments and dances handed from hand to hand, too old or too continuous to date. 337 entries.
