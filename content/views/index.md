# Ways to browse

- [Mosaic](../views/mosaic/index.md): Photo tiles for every region and kind of play; pick a tile to open its games.
- [Atlas](../views/atlas/index.md): Era chapters with photo cards: a journey from the first stone games to today.
- [River of Time](../views/river/index.md): One wide river through six eras, with the cradles as tributaries and games as boats.
- [List](../views/list/index.md): A plain A to Z table with sorting. The simplest, most accessible view.
