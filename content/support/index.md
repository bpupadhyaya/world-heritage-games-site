# Support

Need help with a World Heritage Games app, found a mistake, or want to suggest a game?

- [Report a problem or ask a question](https://github.com/bpupadhyaya/world-heritage-games-site/issues/new) (public issue tracker; please do not post personal information).
- **Restore a purchase:** open the game's About page and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account that bought the unlock.
- **No sound on iPhone:** check the ring/silent switch and the volume.
- **Offline:** every game works without an internet connection.

Policies: [Privacy policy](../privacy/index.md). About the project: [About and method](../about/index.md).
