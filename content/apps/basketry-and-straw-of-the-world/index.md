# Basketry & Straw of the World

A mobile app (iPhone, iPad & Android) with 6 playable basketry and straw-weaving traditions from 5 countries. Live on Google Play. Submitted to the App Store; in review, and the store link appears here the day it goes live.

Version 1.0.0, free to play with ads, one-time purchase to remove ads, works offline.
