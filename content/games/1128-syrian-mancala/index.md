# Syrian mancala

*Also known as: La'b Madjnuni · Madjnuni*

**Encyclopedia ID 1128** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1128-syrian-mancala/

Syrian sowing game; Gaziantep Mangala is said to resemble it.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Syria.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Mesopotamia, Levant and Arabia.
