# Gourd-crab dice

*Bau Cua Ca Cop*

**Encyclopedia ID 1213** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/1213-gourd-crab-dice/

Dice with six symbols; players bet on the face-up symbol, especially at Tet.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Vietnam.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of River Valleys and First Cities (about 3000 to 1000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Southeast Asia and the Islands.
