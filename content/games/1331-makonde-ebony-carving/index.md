# Makonde ebony carving

*Also known as: Makonde*

**Encyclopedia ID 1331** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1331-makonde-ebony-carving/

![Makonde ebony carving](../../../img/photos/01331-makonde-ebony-carving.jpg)

*Photo: Photo by MatthiasKabel, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Makonde_carving_1.jpg)*

Chip and polish a block of blackwood with chisel strokes to reveal a carved figure, following a silhouette guide and keeping grain direction.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Tanzania, Mozambique. Animals are partners in this tradition; good care and local rules always come first.

## Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Africa: Great Lakes and Indian Ocean.
