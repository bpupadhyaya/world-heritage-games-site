# Fujara

*Fujara · Shepherd's Overtone Flute*

**Encyclopedia ID 135** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/135-fujara/

![Fujara](../../../img/photos/00135-fujara.jpg)

*Photo: Palo Čepček, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Palo_Cepcek_Slovakia_25.jpg)*

Blow into the mic or hold to set the breath, press finger holes and pitch overtones to climb a melody; the 2 m tube hums with low drone sounds.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Slovakia.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Classical Worlds (about 1000 BCE to 500 CE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Northern and Eastern Europe.
