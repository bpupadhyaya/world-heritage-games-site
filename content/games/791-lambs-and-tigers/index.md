# Lambs and Tigers

*Also known as: Aadu Puli Attam · Puli Meka · Goats and Tigers*

**Encyclopedia ID 791** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/791-lambs-and-tigers/

Hunt game: three tigers versus up to fifteen goats on a triangle-and-lines board.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of India.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of South Asia.
