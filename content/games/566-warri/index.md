# Warri

*Warri / mancala*

**Encyclopedia ID 566** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/566-warri/

West African mancala brought by enslaved people; now nearly extinct but revived; Speightstown known as Warri capital.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Bahamas, Barbados, Antigua and Barbuda.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of The Americas: Caribbean.
