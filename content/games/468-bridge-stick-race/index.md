# Bridge Stick Race

*Also known as: Poohsticks*

**Encyclopedia ID 468** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/468-bridge-stick-race/

![Bridge Stick Race](../../../img/photos/00468-bridge-stick-race.jpg)
