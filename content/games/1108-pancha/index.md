# Pancha

*Pancha Keliya*

**Encyclopedia ID 1108** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1108-pancha/

Cowrie-shell board race game played in teams at New Year, comparable to Pachisi.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Sri Lanka.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of River Valleys and First Cities (about 3000 to 1000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of South Asia.
