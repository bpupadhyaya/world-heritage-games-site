# Dabuda

*Dabuda · Dabuda I · Dabuda II · Afar mancala*

**Encyclopedia ID 705** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/705-dabuda/

Afar-people mancala described by Richard Pankhurst in 1971, in long and short board versions.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Ethiopia, Djibouti.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Nile and the Horn.
