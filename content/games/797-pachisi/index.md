# Pachisi

*Pachisi / Chaupar (Ludo) · Chaupar · Chausar · Ludo*

**Encyclopedia ID 797** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/797-pachisi/

Cross-shaped race game moved by cowrie or dice throws; its descendant Ludo is the household version.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of India.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of River Valleys and First Cities (about 3000 to 1000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of South Asia.

## Facts

- **Where:** India
- **Type:** Board and table games
- **Family:** Race and cross games
