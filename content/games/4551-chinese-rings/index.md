# Chinese Rings

**Encyclopedia ID 4551** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/4551-chinese-rings/

The classic ring-and-bar puzzle from China. Take three to nine real metal rings on and off a steel bar following one rule, and free them all.

## How it is played

New here? Glowing rings can move right now. Ring 1, next to the free end, always moves.
