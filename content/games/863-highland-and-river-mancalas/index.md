# Highland and river mancalas

*Also known as: Giuthi · Kiothi · Mbothe*

**Encyclopedia ID 863** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/863-highland-and-river-mancalas/

Local mancala forms of Kikuyu/Embu, Meru and Pokomo.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Kenya.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Great Lakes and Indian Ocean.
