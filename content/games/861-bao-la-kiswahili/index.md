# Bao la Kiswahili

*Also known as: Bao*

**Encyclopedia ID 861** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/861-bao-la-kiswahili/

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Kenya, Tanzania.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Great Lakes and Indian Ocean.
