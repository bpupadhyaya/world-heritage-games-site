# Omweso

*Omweso · Mweso*

**Encyclopedia ID 1176** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/1176-omweso/

Baganda national mancala on 4x8 board, once a royal court pastime.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Uganda.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Great Lakes and Indian Ocean.
