# Vitti Dandu

*Also known as: Viti Dandu · Vitti-Dandu · Marathi gilli-danda*

**Encyclopedia ID 9804** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/9804-vitti-dandu/

The Marathi name and local style of gilli-danda, with a small guchhi hole in the centre of the field and its own state federation.

## How it is played

A short pointed stick is levered out of a small hole with a longer stick and struck in flight; the score depends on the distance reached and whether fielders can catch it.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of India.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Age of Contact and Craft (about 1500 to 1800), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of South Asia.
