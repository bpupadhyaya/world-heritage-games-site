# Babatone

*Babatone (one-string bass) · one-string bass*

**Encyclopedia ID 1233** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1233-babatone/

![Babatone](../../../img/photos/01233-babatone.jpg)

*Photo: Stephen and Helen Jones from England, [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gasper_Nali_-_Purbeck_Valley_Folk_Festival_2022_(52319846585).jpg)*

Sit with a tall one-string home-made bass, press a bottle slide along the string and strike or pluck in time with a groove.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Malawi.

## Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Africa: Great Lakes and Indian Ocean.
