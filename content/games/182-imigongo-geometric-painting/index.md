# Imigongo geometric painting

*Imigongo*

**Encyclopedia ID 182** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/182-imigongo-geometric-painting/

![Imigongo geometric painting](../../../img/photos/00182-imigongo-geometric-painting.jpg)

*Photo: Ji-Elle, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Imigongo_traditional_patterns_(3).jpg)*

Lay down raised spiral and chevron ridges on a panel, then colour in the bands in black, white, red and grey so adjacent areas follow the traditional rhythm.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Rwanda. Some forms of this practice carry deep meaning for the communities who keep it; here we describe the play and the craft only, with respect for its roots.

## Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Africa: Great Lakes and Indian Ocean.
