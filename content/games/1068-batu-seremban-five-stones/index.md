# Batu Seremban / Five stones

*Five Stones*

**Encyclopedia ID 1068** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1068-batu-seremban-five-stones/

Players toss one stone and pick up others in graded stages before it lands.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Singapore.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Southeast Asia and the Islands.
