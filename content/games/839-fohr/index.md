# Fohr

*Also known as: Fohr (hidden-piece guessing game)*

**Encyclopedia ID 839** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/839-fohr/

One player hides a piece in a closed hand and the opponent guesses which, moving counters along rings.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Cote d'Ivoire.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Sahel, West and Central.
