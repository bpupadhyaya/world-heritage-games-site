# Al Laqfa

*Also known as: jackstones*

**Encyclopedia ID 1038** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1038-al-laqfa/

Players toss a stone upward and snatch other stones from the ground before catching it.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Qatar.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Mesopotamia, Levant and Arabia.
