# Duo

*Duo · 11-stick alignment game*

**Encyclopedia ID 838** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/838-duo/

Two players move 11 sticks each on a rectangular board to line them up while blocking the opponent.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Cote d'Ivoire.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Sahel, West and Central.
