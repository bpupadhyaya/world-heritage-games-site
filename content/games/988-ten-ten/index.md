# Ten Ten

*Also known as: Ten Ten (Ten-Ten) · Leg lift*

**Encyclopedia ID 988** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/988-ten-ten/

Children face each other and jump to show one or two feet; matching feet score a point, in a rhythm closely related to Ampe.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Nigeria.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Sahel, West and Central.
