# Nonogram

**Encyclopedia ID 4827** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/4827-nonogram/

A calm logic picture puzzle: use the numbers beside the grid to decide which squares are filled and a small illustration appears.

## How it is played

The numbers beside each row and above each column give the lengths of the runs of filled squares in that line. Use Cross to mark squares you know are empty. Think finds the next square that logic proves and explains why. Use Zoom and Move on big boards.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Japan.

## Origins
