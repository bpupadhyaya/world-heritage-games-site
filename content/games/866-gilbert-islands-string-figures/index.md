# Gilbert Islands string figures

*String figures*

**Encyclopedia ID 866** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/866-gilbert-islands-string-figures/

Making patterns by looping string around the fingers, often with chants or stories; a regional tradition assumed here but not confirmed for this country.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Kiribati.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Pacific and Oceania.

## Facts

- **Where:** Kiribati
- **Type:** Dexterity and skill games
- **Family:** Puzzle and dexterity toys

- **Cradle:** [Pacific and Oceania](../../cradles/pacific/index.md)
- **Region:** Oceania and Pacific
- **Players:** 1-2
