# Yam

*Yam*

**Encyclopedia ID 593** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/593-yam/

A dice game played with two six-sided dice and 64 cowrie shells, mostly by men.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Bhutan.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of River Valleys and First Cities (about 3000 to 1000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of South Asia.

- **Where:** Bhutan
- **Type:** Board and table games
