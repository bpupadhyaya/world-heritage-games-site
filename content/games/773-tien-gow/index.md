# Tien gow

*天九 Tin gau · Tin Kau · Heaven nine*

**Encyclopedia ID 773** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/773-tien-gow/

Four-player Chinese dominoes game using 32 tiles in a trick-like structure.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of China.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of China.
