# Koto

**Encyclopedia ID 2937** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/2937-koto/

A calm music game for the Japanese long zither. Slide the ivory bridges to tune thirteen silk strings, pluck with your fingertips, sweep across the strings and press behind a bridge to bend a note upward.

## How it is played

Pick a piece and start with Learn: a short phrase is played, then you play it back. Red discs slide along the strings towards you. A gold ring means press: hold the same string behind its bridge. Close to the bridge bends a half step, further back a whole step.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Japan.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Classical Worlds (about 1000 BCE to 500 CE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Japan and Korea.
