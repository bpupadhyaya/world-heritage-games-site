# Tawle

*Also known as: Shesh Besh · backgammon*

**Encyclopedia ID 880** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/880-tawle/

Two players race fifteen checkers around a 24-point board with dice, a cafe and seafront staple.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Lebanon, Palestine.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of River Valleys and First Cities (about 3000 to 1000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Mesopotamia, Levant and Arabia.
