# Igisoro

*Igisoro · Ikibuguzo*

**Encyclopedia ID 1049** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/1049-igisoro/

Mancala on 4x8 board with 64 seeds meant as cows; masters play blindfolded.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Rwanda, Burundi.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Great Lakes and Indian Ocean.
