# Palant

**Encyclopedia ID 1031** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1031-palant/

A bat-and-ball team game where hitters run between zones and fielders catch or tag them.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Poland.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Northern and Eastern Europe.
