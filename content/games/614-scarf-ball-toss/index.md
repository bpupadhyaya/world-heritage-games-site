# Scarf-ball toss

*Also known as: Chol Chhoung*

**Encyclopedia ID 614** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/614-scarf-ball-toss/

Two teams of boys and girls toss a rolled scarf over a high arc, played on the first night of Khmer New Year.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Cambodia.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Southeast Asia and the Islands.
