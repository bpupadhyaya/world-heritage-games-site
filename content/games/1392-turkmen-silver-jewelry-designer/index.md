# Turkmen Silver Jewelry Designer

*Türkmen şaý-sepleri*

**Encyclopedia ID 1392** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/1392-turkmen-silver-jewelry-designer/

![Turkmen Silver Jewelry Designer](../../../img/photos/01392-turkmen-silver-jewelry-designer.jpg)

*Photo: Gulzar Nurlyyeva, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Handmade_silver_turkmen_jewelry.jpg)*

Arrange silver plates, carnelian cabochons and dangling pendants into a symmetrical bridal-set layout following traditional ornament families.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Turkmenistan. Some forms of this practice carry deep meaning for the communities who keep it; here we describe the play and the craft only, with respect for its roots.

## Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Persia, Caucasus and Central Asia.
