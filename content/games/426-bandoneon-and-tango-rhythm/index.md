# Bandoneon and tango rhythm

*Also known as: Bandoneon*

**Encyclopedia ID 426** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/426-bandoneon-and-tango-rhythm/

![Bandoneon and tango rhythm](../../../img/photos/00426-bandoneon-and-tango-rhythm.jpg)

*Photo: Jorge Royan, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Buenos_Aires_-_Bandoneon_tango_player_-_7435.jpg)*

Squeeze the bellows and press button keys to play tango phrases, timing push and pull notes while the milonga beat runs.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Argentina, Uruguay.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Age of Contact and Craft (about 1500 to 1800), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of The Americas: Andes and South America.
