# Ttakji-ssaum

*Also known as: Orang-kai-kkot-tchi-ki · Violet Fighting*

**Encyclopedia ID 14067** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/14067-ttakji-ssaum/

A children's contest in which flowers of the violet are hooked together and pulled apart so that the head breaks off one.

## How it is played

Each child hooks violets by the stems and tugs; the one whose flower head comes off loses.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of South Korea. It is presented as sport and skill, never as real fighting.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Classical Worlds (about 1000 BCE to 500 CE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Japan and Korea.
