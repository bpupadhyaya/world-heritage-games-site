# Valiha tube zither

*Valiha*

**Encyclopedia ID 124** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/124-valiha-tube-zither/

![Valiha tube zither](../../../img/photos/00124-valiha-tube-zither.jpg)

*Photo: Rob Hooft, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Valiha_player_in_Ambohimahasoa.jpg)*

Pluck strings that run around a bamboo tube with two hands, following falling notes to play melodies, with a free mode of chord layers.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Madagascar.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Classical Worlds (about 1000 BCE to 500 CE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Great Lakes and Indian Ocean.
