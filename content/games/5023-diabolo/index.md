# Diabolo

**Encyclopedia ID 5023** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/5023-diabolo/

A juggling toy of a spool tossed and spun on a string held by two sticks, popular in France and elsewhere in Europe.

## How it is played

The player whips the string between two hand sticks to spin the spool, tossing and catching it in tricks.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of France.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Celtic and Atlantic Europe.
