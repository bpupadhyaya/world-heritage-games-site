# Olinda Keliya

*Also known as: Mancala*

**Encyclopedia ID 1106** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1106-olinda-keliya/

Mancala game played with olinda seeds in pits, where players sow and collect seeds; a New Year pastime.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Sri Lanka.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of South Asia.
