# Perch

**Encyclopedia ID 22335** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/22335-perch/

Perch is an Arcforge original reflex game: a small bird on a thin perch, stones falling from above and one good moment to move.

## How it is played

The bird hops left or right to avoid each falling stone; the longer it survives, the faster the stones come.

## Heritage note

An Arcforge original, created for the World Heritage Games collection rather than drawn from one tradition.
