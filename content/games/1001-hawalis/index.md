# Hawalis

*Hawalis · Omani mancala*

**Encyclopedia ID 1001** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1001-hawalis/

4x7 sowing board played in Oman; Zanzibar cousin Bao la Kiarabu.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Oman.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Mesopotamia, Levant and Arabia.
