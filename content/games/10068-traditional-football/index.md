# Traditional football

*Also known as: Folk football · Mob football · Football · Association football · Soccer*

**Encyclopedia ID 10068** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/10068-traditional-football/

Association football, the world's most played sport, was codified in England in 1863 from older village and school kicking games and is now played in every country.

## How it is played

Two teams move a ball with any part of the body except the hands and arms, trying to kick it into the opposing goal. The full game is eleven a side on a large pitch; seven a side on a compact pitch is the common recreational form.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of United Kingdom.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Celtic and Atlantic Europe.
