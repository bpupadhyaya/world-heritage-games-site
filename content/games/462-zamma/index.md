# Zamma

*Zamma (Zemmat)*

**Encyclopedia ID 462** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/462-zamma/

![Zamma](../../../img/photos/00462-zamma.jpg)

*Photo: Mliu92, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Zamma_55_%2Bpieces.svg)*

Start with 12 pieces each on a 5x5 board, move and capture by jumping with kings added; also played on 7x7 and 9x9 boards.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Morocco.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Mediterranean.
