# Tawla

*Also known as: Nard · Shesh Besh · backgammon*

**Encyclopedia ID 815** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/815-tawla/

Two players race fifteen checkers around a twenty-four point board using dice, hitting blots and bearing off.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Iraq, Syria, Jordan.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of River Valleys and First Cities (about 3000 to 1000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Mesopotamia, Levant and Arabia.
