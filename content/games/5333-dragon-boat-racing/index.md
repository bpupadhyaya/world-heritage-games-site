# Dragon boat racing

*Also known as: 龙舟竞渡*

**Encyclopedia ID 5333** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/5333-dragon-boat-racing/

![Dragon boat racing](../../../img/photos/05333-dragon-boat-racing.jpg)

*Photo: Deerpeter, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Drakenboot_Giethoorn.jpg)*

Long narrow paddled boats with a carved dragon head race in teams, set to a drummer's beat.

## How it is played

Teams of paddlers paddle in unison to a drum while a steersperson guides the boat down a river course.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of China.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of China.
