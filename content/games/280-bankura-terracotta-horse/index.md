# Bankura terracotta horse

*বাঁকুড়া ঘোড়া*

**Encyclopedia ID 280** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/280-bankura-terracotta-horse/

![Bankura terracotta horse](../../../img/photos/00280-bankura-terracotta-horse.jpg)

*Photo: Footlooseinme, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3ABankura_Terracotta_Horses.jpg)*

Build a horse from hand-formed hollow clay parts, decorate with stamps, and fire it in a village kiln.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Bangladesh. Some forms of this practice carry deep meaning for the communities who keep it; here we describe the play and the craft only, with respect for its roots. Animals are partners in this tradition; good care and local rules always come first.

## Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of South Asia.
