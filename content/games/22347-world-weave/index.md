# World Weave

**Encyclopedia ID 22347** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/22347-world-weave/

World Weave is an Arcforge original pattern puzzle that weaves real textile and tile traditions from many civilizations into one loom.

## How it is played

Each pattern is rebuilt thread by thread from the motifs of its home tradition, following the lineage of the design rather than any one country.

## Heritage note

An Arcforge original, created for the World Heritage Games collection rather than drawn from one tradition.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today).
