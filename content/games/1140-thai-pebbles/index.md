# Thai pebbles

*Mak Kep · Mak kep*

**Encyclopedia ID 1140** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1140-thai-pebbles/

Toss one stone while collecting others in stages.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Thailand.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Southeast Asia and the Islands.
