# Shisima

*Also known as: Water bug game*

**Encyclopedia ID 865** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/865-shisima/

Three-piece octagon board game of the Luhya, similar to tic-tac-toe with moving pieces.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Kenya.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Great Lakes and Indian Ocean.
