# Samoan cricket

*Also known as: Kilikiti*

**Encyclopedia ID 1056** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1056-samoan-cricket/

Community cricket using a three-sided wooden club and pandanus ball, with singing, food and village hosting.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Samoa.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Age of Contact and Craft (about 1500 to 1800), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Pacific and Oceania.

- **Where:** Samoa
