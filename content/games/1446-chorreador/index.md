# Chorreador

*Also known as: Chorreador de café · Costa Rican drip coffee*

**Encyclopedia ID 1446** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1446-chorreador/

![Chorreador](../../../img/photos/01446-chorreador.jpg)

*Photo: Sharalds, [Public domain](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chorreador.jpg)*

Hang the cloth bag on the wooden stand, add ground coffee, pour hot water slowly and judge the pour for strength and timing.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Costa Rica.

## Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of The Americas: Mesoamerica.
