# Whai

*Also known as: Maori string figures*

**Encyclopedia ID 982** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/982-whai/

Pattern making with a flax loop, accompanied by chants; traditionally played by all ages.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of New Zealand.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Pacific and Oceania.
