# Tiger and Goat

*Bagh-Chal · Bagh Chal · Bagha-chall*

**Encyclopedia ID 967** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/967-tiger-and-goat/

Four tigers versus twenty goats on a 5x5 grid.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Nepal.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of South Asia.
