# Nsolo

*Also known as: Isolo · Insolo · Chisolo*

**Encyclopedia ID 1220** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1220-nsolo/

Large four-row mancala dug in the ground; some boards up to 4x36.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Zambia, Malawi.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Great Lakes and Indian Ocean.
