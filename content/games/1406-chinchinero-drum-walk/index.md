# Chinchinero Drum Walk

*Chinchinero*

**Encyclopedia ID 1406** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/1406-chinchinero-drum-walk/

![Chinchinero Drum Walk](../../../img/photos/01406-chinchinero-drum-walk.jpg)

*Photo: Carlos Ebert from São Paulo, BrazilGRU, [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chinchineros_-_man_orchestra_(4306187131).jpg)*

A street performer carries a big bass drum on his back, beats it with sticks while dancing and sometimes plays cymbals with his feet; the player copies drum patterns and steps.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Chile.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of The Americas: Andes and South America.
