# Kalamkari pen painting

*Also known as: కలంకారి*

**Encyclopedia ID 273** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/273-kalamkari-pen-painting/

![Kalamkari pen painting](../../../img/photos/00273-kalamkari-pen-painting.jpg)

*Photo: Ravitheja Kumar Reddy C, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3AKalamkari_Art.jpg)*

Draw natural motifs with a bamboo kalam on cloth, then fill in them using the mordant-and-dye steps in the right order.

## Heritage note

A tradition shared by communities in many places.

## Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin.
