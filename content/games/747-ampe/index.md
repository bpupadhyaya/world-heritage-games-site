# Ampe

*Ampe*

**Encyclopedia ID 747** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/747-ampe/

Children's jump-and-clap game: both players jump, clap and thrust a foot; if the feet match the leader scores.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Ghana, Togo.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Sahel, West and Central.
