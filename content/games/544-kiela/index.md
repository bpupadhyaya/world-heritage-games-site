# Kiela

*Kiela · Kimbundu mancala*

**Encyclopedia ID 544** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/544-kiela/

Kimbundu mancala of northern Angola, called a game of peace; similar to Omweso.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Angola.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Southern Africa.
