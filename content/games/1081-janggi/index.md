# Janggi

*장기 Janggi · Korean chess*

**Encyclopedia ID 1081** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1081-janggi/

Chess-like game on a 9x10 board without a river, where the general starts in the palace centre and players may pass.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of South Korea, North Korea.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Japan and Korea.
