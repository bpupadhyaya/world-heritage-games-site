# Draughts

*Drafts*

**Encyclopedia ID 576** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/576-draughts/

African draughts, normally the 10x10 international form with flying kings, played on street boards, in cafes and in federation events.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Barbados, Antigua and Barbuda, Saint Lucia.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of The Americas: Caribbean.
