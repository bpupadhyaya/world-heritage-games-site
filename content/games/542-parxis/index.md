# Parxís

*Also known as: Catalan Parchís*

**Encyclopedia ID 542** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/542-parxis/

Players race four pieces around a cross-shaped track by dice, with captures and safe squares.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Andorra.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of River Valleys and First Cities (about 3000 to 1000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Mediterranean.
