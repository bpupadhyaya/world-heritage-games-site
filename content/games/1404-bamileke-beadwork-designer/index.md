# Bamileke beadwork designer

**Encyclopedia ID 1404** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1404-bamileke-beadwork-designer/

![Bamileke beadwork designer](../../../img/photos/01404-bamileke-beadwork-designer.jpg)

*Photo: Ann Porteus from Tasmania, Australia, [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:4924_Bamileke_beaded_bag._Cameroon_(3420342066).jpg)*

String and place coloured glass beads and cowries onto a panel following traditional geometric motifs to fill a bag, stool cover or calabash.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Cameroon. Some forms of this practice carry deep meaning for the communities who keep it; here we describe the play and the craft only, with respect for its roots.

## Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Africa: Sahel, West and Central.
