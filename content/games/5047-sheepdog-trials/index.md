# Sheepdog trials

**Encyclopedia ID 5047** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/5047-sheepdog-trials/

Competitions of herding dogs guiding sheep around a field course through gates and into a pen on the shepherd's calls.

## How it is played

The handler directs a dog by whistles and calls to move a small flock through a set course against the clock.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of United Kingdom. Animals are partners in this tradition; good care and local rules always come first.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Classical Worlds (about 1000 BCE to 500 CE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Celtic and Atlantic Europe.
