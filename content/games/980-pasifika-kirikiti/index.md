# Pasifika kirikiti

*Also known as: Kirikiti · diaspora*

**Encyclopedia ID 980** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/980-pasifika-kirikiti/

Samoan/Pacific-style cricket played in NZ Pacific communities at community gatherings.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of New Zealand.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Age of Contact and Craft (about 1500 to 1800), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Pacific and Oceania.
