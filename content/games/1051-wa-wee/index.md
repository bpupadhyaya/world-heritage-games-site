# Wa-wee

*Also known as: Wa-wee (English Wari)*

**Encyclopedia ID 1051** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1051-wa-wee/

Two-row mancala documented in early 20th-century St Lucia, boards sold in Castries.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Saint Lucia.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of The Americas: Caribbean.
