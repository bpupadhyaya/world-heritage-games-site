# Varpa

*varpa · Gotland throwing game*

**Encyclopedia ID 1396** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1396-varpa/

![Varpa](../../../img/photos/01396-varpa.jpg)

*Photo: Bene Riobó, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Varpa_en_Kr%C3%A4klingbo_23.jpg)*

Throw a flat heavy disc 15-20 m to land beside a stick, trading turns with an opponent.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Sweden. Animals are partners in this tradition; good care and local rules always come first.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Northern and Eastern Europe.
