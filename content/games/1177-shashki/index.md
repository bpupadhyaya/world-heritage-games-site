# Shashki

*Шашки · Russian draughts*

**Encyclopedia ID 1177** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/1177-shashki/

8x8 draughts with flying kings and backwards captures; Poddavki and Bashni are popular variants.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Ukraine, Belarus, Russia.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Northern and Eastern Europe.
