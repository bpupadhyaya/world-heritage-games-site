# Kurai

*Also known as: Ҡурай · Bashkir reed flute*

**Encyclopedia ID 1237** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1237-kurai/

![Kurai](../../../img/photos/01237-kurai.jpg)

*Photo: Vyacheslav Kirillin, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Quray_(2022-10-04).jpg)*

Blow across the open reed tube while covering and uncovering holes to play drawn-out melodies.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Russia. Some forms of this practice carry deep meaning for the communities who keep it; here we describe the play and the craft only, with respect for its roots.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Classical Worlds (about 1000 BCE to 500 CE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Northern and Eastern Europe.
