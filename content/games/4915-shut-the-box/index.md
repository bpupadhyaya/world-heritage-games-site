# Shut the Box

**Encyclopedia ID 4915** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/4915-shut-the-box/

![Shut the Box](../../../img/photos/04915-shut-the-box.jpg)

*Photo: Roland Scheicher / Roland Scheicher at German Wikipedia, [Public domain](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Shut_the_box.jpg)*

The classic tavern dice game of England and France on a lit oak box with brass hinges and bone tiles.

## How it is played

When they match, they flip down by themselves. Keep rolling and shutting. If no open tiles can make the roll, the box is over.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of France.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of River Valleys and First Cities (about 3000 to 1000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Celtic and Atlantic Europe.
