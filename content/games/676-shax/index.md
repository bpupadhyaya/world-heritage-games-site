# Shax

*Shax · Jar · Shah*

**Encyclopedia ID 676** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/676-shax/

Mill-style placement-then-movement game drawn on the ground; poetry and clan lore cite it.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Djibouti, Somalia.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of River Valleys and First Cities (about 3000 to 1000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Nile and the Horn.
