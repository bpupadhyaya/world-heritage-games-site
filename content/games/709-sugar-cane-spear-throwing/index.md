# Sugar-cane spear throwing

*Vakadovu*

**Encyclopedia ID 709** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/709-sugar-cane-spear-throwing/

Throwers stand at a distance and aim spears at a heap of cut sugar cane; first to hit wins.

## How it is played

Throwers stand at a distance and aim spears at a heap of cut sugar cane; first to hit wins. Practised on Kabara, Lau.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Fiji.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Pacific and Oceania.
