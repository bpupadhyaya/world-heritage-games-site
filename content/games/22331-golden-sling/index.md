# Golden Sling

*Also known as: Harvest Sling · Golden Sling Deluxe*

**Encyclopedia ID 22331** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/22331-golden-sling/

Golden Sling is an Arcforge original: a harvest field, a hand-carved slingshot and a flock of birds to outwit before they strip the crop. Four seasons bring four guardians to a paper-cut landscape.

## How it is played

Stones are launched from the sling to scatter the birds before they reach the standing grain; each season changes the wind, the light and the flock.

## Heritage note

An Arcforge original, created for the World Heritage Games collection rather than drawn from one tradition.

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today).
