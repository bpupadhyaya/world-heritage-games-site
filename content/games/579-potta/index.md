# Potta

*Also known as: Union Jack Game (Antigua) · Morale (France) · three-in-a-row line game*

**Encyclopedia ID 579** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/579-potta/

Players place and move three pieces on a board of intersecting lines aiming to line them up, a cousin of nine-men's-morris style games.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Barbados.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of River Valleys and First Cities (about 3000 to 1000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of The Americas: Caribbean.
