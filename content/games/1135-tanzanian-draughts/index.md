# Tanzanian draughts

*Drafti*

**Encyclopedia ID 1135** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1135-tanzanian-draughts/

8x8 draughts with flying kings (a hybrid of American and Russian rules).

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Tanzania.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Great Lakes and Indian Ocean.
