# Ohvalhu

*Also known as: Ohvalhu Gondi · Oh’valhu-gondi*

**Encyclopedia ID 916** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/916-ohvalhu/

A small mancala on an eight-pit board where players sow shells counter-clockwise to fill a home cup.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Maldives.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of South Asia.
