# American football

*Also known as: Gridiron football*

**Encyclopedia ID 22320** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/22320-american-football/

American football grew out of rugby and association football on the college fields of the north-eastern United States in the 1870s, and became the country's most watched sport.

## How it is played

Two teams advance an oval ball down a 100-yard field by running or passing, needing ten yards in four downs to keep possession; a touchdown is worth six points and a field goal three. Seven-on-seven is the non-contact practice form, all passing, played on a short field.
