# Baibalaa

**Encyclopedia ID 914** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/914-baibalaa/

Players in a ring field tag and capture opponents, much like kabaddi.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Maldives.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of South Asia.

## Facts

- **Where:** Maldives
- **Type:** Street and playground games
- **Family:** Tag, chase and hide games
- **Era:** [Deep Past and First Games](../../eras/deep-past/index.md) (uncertain)
- **Cradle:** [South Asia](../../cradles/south-asia/index.md)
- **Region:** South and Central Asia
- **Players:** 2 teams
