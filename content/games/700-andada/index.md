# Andada

*Also known as: Kunama mancala*

**Encyclopedia ID 700** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/700-andada/

Mancala of the Kunama of western Eritrea where the last mover wins by leaving the opponent without counters.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Eritrea.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Nile and the Horn.
