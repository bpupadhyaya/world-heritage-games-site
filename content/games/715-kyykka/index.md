# Kyykka

*Also known as: Finnish skittles*

**Encyclopedia ID 715** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/715-kyykka/

Throw flat bats to clear skittles from a 5x5 m square in as few throws as possible.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Finland.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Age of Contact and Craft (about 1500 to 1800), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Northern and Eastern Europe.
