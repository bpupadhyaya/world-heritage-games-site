# Tavla

*Also known as: Табла*

**Encyclopedia ID 992** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/992-tavla/

Two players race fifteen checkers each around a board with dice, hitting blots; the Balkan form of backgammon.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of North Macedonia, Montenegro.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of River Valleys and First Cities (about 3000 to 1000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Mediterranean.
