# Pai gow

*牌九 Paai gau*

**Encyclopedia ID 771** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/771-pai-gow/

Players split four dominoes into a front and back hand and beat the banker's two hands.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of China.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of China.
