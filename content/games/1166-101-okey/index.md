# 101 Okey

*Yüzbir Okey · Okey 101*

**Encyclopedia ID 1166** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/1166-101-okey/

Okey variant with melding during play and point penalties across rounds.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Turkey.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Mesopotamia, Levant and Arabia.
