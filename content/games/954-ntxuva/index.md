# Ntxuva

*Also known as: Tchadji · Mancala · Ntchuva*

**Encyclopedia ID 954** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/954-ntxuva/

Four-row mancala dug in ground or cement boards, played by adult men nationwide.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Mozambique.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Southern Africa.
