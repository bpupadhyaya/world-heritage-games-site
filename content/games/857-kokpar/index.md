# Kokpar

*Also known as: goat-carcass polo*

**Encyclopedia ID 857** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/857-kokpar/

Mounted teams fight to carry a goat carcass into the opposing goal; an official sport with national federations.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Kazakhstan.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Classical Worlds (about 1000 BCE to 500 CE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Persia, Caucasus and Central Asia.
