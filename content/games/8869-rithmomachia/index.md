# Rithmomachia

*Also known as: Philosophers' game*

**Encyclopedia ID 8869** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/8869-rithmomachia/

Medieval European mathematical board game in which captures depend on the numbers inscribed on pieces.

## How it is played

Pieces with numbers capture by arithmetic relations and a win comes from forming a victory progression.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Italy.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Mediterranean.
