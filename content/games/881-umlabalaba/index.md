# Umlabalaba

*Umlabalaba · Zulu chess · Morabaraba (Twelve Cows) · Mlabalaba*

**Encyclopedia ID 881** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/881-umlabalaba/

Mill-style cattle capture game, stated as most popular board game in Lesotho, Swaziland and KwaZulu-Natal.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Lesotho, Eswatini.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of River Valleys and First Cities (about 3000 to 1000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Southern Africa.

## Facts

- **Where:** Lesotho, Eswatini, South Africa, Botswana
- **Type:** Board and table games
- **Family:** Mill and morris games
- **Era:** [River Valleys and First Cities](../../eras/river-valleys/index.md) (uncertain)
