# Snakes and Ladders

*Also known as: Moksha Patam · Saap Seedhi · Gyan Chaupar · Saanp Seedhi*

**Encyclopedia ID 796** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/796-snakes-and-ladders/

Dice race up a numbered grid, with ladders to climb and snakes to slide down.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of India.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of River Valleys and First Cities (about 3000 to 1000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of South Asia.
