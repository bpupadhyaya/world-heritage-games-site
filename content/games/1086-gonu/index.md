# Gonu

*고누 Gonu · Gono*

**Encyclopedia ID 1086** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1086-gonu/

Family of small line-and-capture games played with pebbles on lines scratched on the ground.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of South Korea.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Japan and Korea.
