# Paduk

*Also known as: Baduk · Go*

**Encyclopedia ID 990** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/990-paduk/

North Korea's name for go; organised nationally and treated as a 'mental martial art' under the Taekwondo organisation.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of North Korea.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Classical Worlds (about 1000 BCE to 500 CE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Japan and Korea.
