# Taba

*Taba · knucklebone toss*

**Encyclopedia ID 594** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/594-taba/

Two players race fifteen checkers each around a board with dice, hitting blots; the Bulgarian form of backgammon.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Bolivia, Bulgaria.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of The Americas: Andes and South America.
