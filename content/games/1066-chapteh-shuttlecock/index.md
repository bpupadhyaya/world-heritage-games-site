# Chapteh shuttlecock

*Capteh (Chapteh)*

**Encyclopedia ID 1066** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1066-chapteh-shuttlecock/

Players keep a feathered, weighted shuttle in the air with the feet, a pastime for children that leads into sepak raga.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Singapore.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Southeast Asia and the Islands.
