# Gaple dominoes

*Also known as: Gaple*

**Encyclopedia ID 804** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/804-gaple-dominoes/

Domino matching game with a 28-tile set, played in warungs and at night watches; strongly social.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Indonesia.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Southeast Asia and the Islands.
