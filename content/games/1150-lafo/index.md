# Lafo

*Lafo*

**Encyclopedia ID 1150** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1150-lafo/

A mat game with small discs or seeds that appears in a Vava'u photograph from 1895; rules are not verified here.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Tonga.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Pacific and Oceania.
