# Mill

*Also known as: Jeu du moulin (Mill)*

**Encyclopedia ID 1061** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1061-mill/

Three-in-a-row mill game on concentric squares.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Senegal.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of River Valleys and First Cities (about 3000 to 1000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Sahel, West and Central.
