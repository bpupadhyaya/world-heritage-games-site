# Don't Get Mad

*Also known as: Mos u Nxeh*

**Encyclopedia ID 530** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/530-dont-get-mad/

Albanian version of the Ludo-type race game, popular under communism.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Albania.

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Mediterranean.
