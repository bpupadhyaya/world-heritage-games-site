# Kite fighting

*Also known as: Patang Baazi (Kite fighting) · Kite flying · Manja*

**Encyclopedia ID 788** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/788-kite-fighting/

Fliers use abrasive-coated string to cut rivals' kites mid-air, then chase the fallen kites; huge around festival days.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of India, Pakistan, Afghanistan. It is presented as sport and skill, never as real fighting.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Classical Worlds (about 1000 BCE to 500 CE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of South Asia.

## Facts

- **Where:** India, Pakistan, Afghanistan
- **Type:** Dexterity and skill games
- **Family:** Tops, kites and spinning toys
- **Era:** [Classical Worlds](../../eras/classical/index.md) (uncertain)
