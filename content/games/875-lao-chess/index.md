# Lao chess

*Also known as: Mak Ruk (Lao chess)*

**Encyclopedia ID 875** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/875-lao-chess/

Lao form of the Makruk-family chess; rules follow the Thai game closely.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Laos.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Southeast Asia and the Islands.
