# Sig

*Also known as: Siga*

**Encyclopedia ID 928** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/928-sig/

Women's afternoon game where two teams move pieces along a sand-hill track using thrown coloured sticks.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Mauritania.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Sahel, West and Central.
