# Alchik

*Alchik (Chuko)*

**Encyclopedia ID 868** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/868-alchik/

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Kyrgyzstan.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Persia, Caucasus and Central Asia.
