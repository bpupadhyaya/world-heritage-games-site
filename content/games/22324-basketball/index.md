# Basketball

*Also known as: 3x3 basketball*

**Encyclopedia ID 22324** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/22324-basketball/

Basketball was invented in 1891 in Springfield, Massachusetts, as an indoor winter game with a ball and two peach baskets, and spread from school gyms to city courts around the world.

## How it is played

Teams score by shooting the ball through a hoop three metres above the floor; players advance by passing or dribbling and may not run holding the ball. Half-court three-on-three, with one basket and a twelve-second shot clock, is the street form and an Olympic event since 2020.
