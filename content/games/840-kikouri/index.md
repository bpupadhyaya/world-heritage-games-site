# Kikouri

*Kikouri · stick strategy game*

**Encyclopedia ID 840** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/840-kikouri/

A game from Gagnoa played with sticks on a simple board to build concentration.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Cote d'Ivoire.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Sahel, West and Central.
