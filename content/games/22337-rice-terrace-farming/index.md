# Rice terrace farming

*Also known as: Ifugao rice terraces · Subak*

**Encyclopedia ID 22337** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/22337-rice-terrace-farming/

The stone-walled rice terraces of the Philippine Cordillera and the shared-water rice fields of Bali are communal works of engineering: spring water is led downhill through channels and gates so that every field floods and drains in its turn.

## How it is played

Farmers follow a calendar of planting, flooding, weeding and harvest set by the community, and the irrigation council of each valley decides when each group of fields may take water.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Philippines, Indonesia.

## Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Southeast Asia and the Islands.
