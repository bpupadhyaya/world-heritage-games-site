# Akedewama

*Also known as: Akedewama (Spider game)*

**Encyclopedia ID 837** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/837-akedewama/

Agni and Baoule three-in-a-row game on a four-sided board with a central point.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Cote d'Ivoire.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Sahel, West and Central.
