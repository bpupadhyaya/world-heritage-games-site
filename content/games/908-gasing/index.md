# Gasing

*Gasing · spinning top*

**Encyclopedia ID 908** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/908-gasing/

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Malaysia, Singapore, Brunei.
