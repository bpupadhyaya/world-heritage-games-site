# Bashi

**Encyclopedia ID 915** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/915-bashi/

![Bashi](../../../img/photos/00915-bashi.jpg)

*Photo: Leeturtle, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kishi_Bashi_at_Rockwave_Festival_2023.jpg)*

Women's sand-court game: a server hits the ball backwards over a net and head, and opponents must catch it; teams of 7 or 11.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Maldives.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of South Asia.

## Facts

- **Where:** Maldives
- **Type:** Street and playground games

- **Cradle:** [South Asia](../../cradles/south-asia/index.md)
- **Region:** South and Central Asia
- **Players:** teams of 7 or 11
