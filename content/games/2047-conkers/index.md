# Conkers

**Encyclopedia ID 2047** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/2047-conkers/

![Conkers](../../../img/photos/02047-conkers.jpg)

England's autumn playground game with real pendulum physics.

## How it is played

Press on the scene, drag back and let go to swing your conker. Raise or lower your hand to hit their pale patch: the soft spot. Watch the guide and the timing strip, then release at a good moment.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of United Kingdom.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Celtic and Atlantic Europe.
