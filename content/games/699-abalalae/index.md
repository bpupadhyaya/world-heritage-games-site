# Abalala'e

*Abalala'e / Gebeta · Gebeta · Gabata · Abalala'e · Eritrean highland mancala*

**Encyclopedia ID 699** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/699-abalalae/

The mancala most widely played in the Eritrean highlands; the name means eating in Tigrinya.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Eritrea.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Nile and the Horn.

## Facts

- **Where:** Eritrea
- **Type:** Board and table games
- **Family:** Mancala and sowing games
- **Era:** [Deep Past and First Games](../../eras/deep-past/index.md) (uncertain)
- **Cradle:** [Africa: Nile and the Horn](../../cradles/africa-nile/index.md)
- **Region:** Sub-Saharan Africa
- **Players:** 2
