# Ayo

*Also known as: Ayo Olopon · Ayoayo · Ncho · Okwe · Nsa Isong · Ogirise*

**Encyclopedia ID 986** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/986-ayo/

Yoruba sowing and capture game on a 2x6 wooden board with 48 seeds; played across Nigeria under many names.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Nigeria.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Sahel, West and Central.

## Facts

- **Where:** Nigeria
