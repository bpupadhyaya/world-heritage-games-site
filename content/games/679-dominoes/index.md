# Dominoes

*Dominó · double-six*

**Encyclopedia ID 679** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/679-dominoes/

Players match tile ends from a double-six set in two- to four-player rounds, a fixture of coffeehouse tables across the Arab world.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Dominican Republic, Trinidad and Tobago, Antigua and Barbuda.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of The Americas: Caribbean.

## Facts

- **Where:** Dominican Republic, Trinidad and Tobago, Antigua and Barbuda, Saint Lucia, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Grenada
