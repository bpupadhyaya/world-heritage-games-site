# Sija

*Also known as: Chadian mancala*

**Encyclopedia ID 627** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/627-sija/

Traditional African mancala-family sowing game; exact local rules vary by village.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Chad.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Sahel, West and Central.
