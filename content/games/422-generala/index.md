# Generala

*Generala · five-dice cup game*

**Encyclopedia ID 422** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/422-generala/

![Generala](../../../img/photos/00422-generala.jpg)

*Photo: Daniel Dionne, [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dice_(5330056727).jpg)*

Roll five dice in a cup up to three times, keeping some, and fill a score sheet of straights, full houses, fours and the big 'generala' (five of a kind).

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of River Valleys and First Cities (about 3000 to 1000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of The Americas: Andes and South America.
