# Ouril

*Also known as: Ouril (Uril) · Ouri · Uril · Urim · Ori*

**Encyclopedia ID 611** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/611-ouril/

Oware-like sowing game with local capture rules; most played in Sao Vicente, played in squares and port towns, mainly by men.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Cape Verde, Guinea-Bissau.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Sahel, West and Central.
