# Lithuanian Durak variants

*Also known as: Durnius · Kopšas · Vežimas*

**Encyclopedia ID 894** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/894-lithuanian-durak-variants/

Three local Durak-family beating games; Kopšas matches Podkidnoy and Vežimas matches Prostoy Durak.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Lithuania.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Age of Contact and Craft (about 1500 to 1800), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Northern and Eastern Europe.
