# Jackstraws

*Also known as: Eskimo and Haida*

**Encyclopedia ID 14110** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/14110-jackstraws/

A game of splints piled on the back of the hand, found among Eskimo and Haida, played like jackstraws.

## How it is played

Players pile fifty to seventy-five small wooden splints and remove them one at a time without disturbing the rest.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of United States.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of The Americas: North America.
