# Portuguese staff fighting

*Also known as: Jogo do Pau*

**Encyclopedia ID 1037** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1037-portuguese-staff-fighting/

Stick-fencing art from northern Portugal with set strikes and parries, now a sport.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Portugal. It is presented as sport and skill, never as real fighting.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Classical Worlds (about 1000 BCE to 500 CE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Mediterranean.
