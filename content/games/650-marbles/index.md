# Marbles

*Also known as: Las Bolas · Kancha · Kelereng · Billes · Canicas*

**Encyclopedia ID 650** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/650-marbles/

Players knock marbles out of a drawn circle or triangle and keep those they win, using named types such as neya and miami.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Cuba, Jamaica, Barbados.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of The Americas: Caribbean.
