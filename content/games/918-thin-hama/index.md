# Thin Hama

*Thin Hama*

**Encyclopedia ID 918** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/918-thin-hama/

Two players place and move pieces on a marked board to line up three in a row; rule detail unclear (morris-like).

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Maldives.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of River Valleys and First Cities (about 3000 to 1000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of South Asia.
