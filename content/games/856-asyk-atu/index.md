# Asyk Atu

*Also known as: Asyq atu · Asyk · Asyq · Sheep-ankle-bone hitting game*

**Encyclopedia ID 856** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/856-asyk-atu/

Players flick or throw a heavy ankle bone to knock opponents' bones out of a line or ring, and keep what they hit.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Kazakhstan.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Persia, Caucasus and Central Asia.
