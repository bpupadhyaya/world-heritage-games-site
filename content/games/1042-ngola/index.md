# Ngola

*Ngola · Congolese mancala*

**Encyclopedia ID 1042** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1042-ngola/

Two rows of six holes, 44 pieces, played at home and in the street; described as African chess in Congolese press.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Congo.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Sahel, West and Central.
