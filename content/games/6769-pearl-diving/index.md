# Pearl diving

*Also known as: الغوص · Ghaus · ghaws*

**Encyclopedia ID 6769** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/6769-pearl-diving/

Hand-diving for pearl oysters, a main seasonal industry of the Gulf before oil.

## How it is played

Divers with nose clip and stone weight descend and collect oysters while hauled up by a rope partner.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Bahrain.

## Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Mesopotamia, Levant and Arabia.
