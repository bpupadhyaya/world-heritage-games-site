# Sipa

*Sipa*

**Encyclopedia ID 1024** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1024-sipa/

Kick a washer-and-straw shuttle to keep it airborne; formerly the national sport until 2009.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Philippines.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Southeast Asia and the Islands.
