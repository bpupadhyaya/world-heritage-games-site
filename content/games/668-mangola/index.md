# Mangola

*Mangola*

**Encyclopedia ID 668** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/668-mangola/

Mancala game described as the most popular mancala in DR Congo, developed after 1911 and replacing older games.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of DR Congo.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Deep Past and First Games (before about 3000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Sahel, West and Central.

## Facts

- **Where:** DR Congo
- **Type:** Board and table games
- **Family:** Mancala and sowing games
