# Bouzouki Strum

*Μπουζούκι*

**Encyclopedia ID 1247** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/1247-bouzouki-strum/

![Bouzouki Strum](../../../img/photos/01247-bouzouki-strum.jpg)

*Photo: Udi Cohen (Udico), [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bouzouki_player.jpg)*

Pluck paired steel strings in a quick tremolo as notes fall, shifting between rebetiko scale modes.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Greece.

## Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Mediterranean.
