# Dudo / Cacho

*Cacho / Dudo*

**Encyclopedia ID 1020** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/1020-dudo-cacho/

Players secretly roll dice under cups and bid on how many of a face are on the table, challenging bluffs.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Peru.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of River Valleys and First Cities (about 3000 to 1000 BCE), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of The Americas: Andes and South America.
