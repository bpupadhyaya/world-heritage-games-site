# Bolga Basket Weaver

*Bolga baskets*

**Encyclopedia ID 1245** (permanent) · https://equalinformation.com/world-heritage-games-site/games/1245-bolga-basket-weaver/

![Bolga Basket Weaver](../../../img/photos/01245-bolga-basket-weaver.jpg)

*Photo: Dnshitobu, [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Weaving_buskets_at_the_Bolga_market.jpg)*

Coil and weave dyed elephant-grass strands into baskets, matching size and colour patterns to market orders as you build up quick stacks.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Ghana.

## Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Africa: Sahel, West and Central.
