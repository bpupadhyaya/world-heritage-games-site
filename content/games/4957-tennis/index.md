# Tennis

*Also known as: Lawn tennis*

**Encyclopedia ID 4957** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/4957-tennis/

Lawn tennis, born in England, in real 3D. Press and drag on the court to choose where the ball lands, release as the ring closes, and play games, sets and tiebreaks on lawn, clay or hard courts.

## How it is played

Your player runs to the ball; you choose the shot. Press and drag on the court: far up is a drive, short is a drop, down is a lob. Release as the ring closes for a clean strike.

## Heritage note

A tradition shared by communities in many places.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Modern Heritage (1800 to today), although its exact origins are uncertain.
