# Crossword

**Encyclopedia ID 22326** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/22326-crossword/

The crossword puzzle first appeared in a New York newspaper in 1913 and within a decade was a daily habit in Britain and America, where the cryptic style developed.

## How it is played

A grid of white and black squares is filled with words running across and down, each answering a numbered clue. American grids are dense and fully interlocking; British cryptic clues hide the answer in wordplay as well as a definition.
