# Miniature golf

*Also known as: Mini golf · Crazy golf · Putting course*

**Encyclopedia ID 22333** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/22333-miniature-golf/

Miniature golf shrinks the putting green into a course of short holes with banks, tunnels and water, a seaside and roadside pastime since the early twentieth century.

## How it is played

Players putt a ball from a tee to a hole on each of nine or eighteen small greens, counting strokes; obstacles and slopes make each hole a small physics problem.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of United Kingdom, United States.

## Origins
