# Choko

**Encyclopedia ID 733** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/733-choko/

Capture game on a 5x5 board with 12 pieces each, attributed to Mandinka and Fula players in the Gambia valley.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Gambia.

## Origins

Usually placed in the era of Crossroads and Golden Ages (about 500 to 1500), although its exact origins are uncertain, within the cultural world of Africa: Sahel, West and Central.
