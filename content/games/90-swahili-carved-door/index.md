# Swahili carved door

*Mlango wa Zanzibar*

**Encyclopedia ID 90** (permanent) · https://bpupadhyaya.github.io/world-heritage-games-site/games/90-swahili-carved-door/

![Swahili carved door](../../../img/photos/00090-swahili-carved-door.jpg)

*Photo: Eric Kilby, [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/), [source on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ornate_Carved_Door_in_Zanzibar.jpg)*

Chisel flowing floral and chain motifs into a wooden door, following guide lines, then fit brass studs and hang the door.

## Heritage note

Part of the living heritage of Kenya.

## Origins

A living tradition passed from person to person; it is not tied to a single dated origin. It belongs to the cultural world of Africa: Great Lakes and Indian Ocean.
