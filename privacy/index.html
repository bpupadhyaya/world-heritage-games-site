Privacy policy for the World Heritage Games apps

Last updated: 12 October 2026.

Short version: the apps collect no personal data.

What the apps do and do not do

  • The games run entirely on your device and work offline. They need no account and no sign-in.
  • We do not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information.
  • There are no advertisements, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your best scores, settings (sound, text size) and whether you have unlocked the app are kept only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We receive no payment details and no information about you. The store tells the app only whether the one-time unlock has been bought, so that Restore purchase works.
  • The apps ask for no access to your contacts, photos, location or camera. Where a game offers a microphone feature it is optional, and sound is processed on the device only.

Children

The apps contain no ads, no links out to the web without your tap, and no chat or social features.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above. Questions: open an issue or use the support page.
